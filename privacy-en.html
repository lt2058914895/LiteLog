Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

1. Local data processing

LiteLog is designed with a local-first approach. Weight, body fat, circumference, goals, nickname, and avatar data are stored in the app sandbox on your device. This version does not connect to a LiteLog server or upload, sell, or share this information.

2. Photos and avatar

Record photos are not supported. You can choose an avatar from the system photo library. The processed image is stored only in the app sandbox and protected by iOS file encryption.

3. Accounts and tracking

LiteLog does not create accounts, collect device identifiers, or include third-party advertising or behavioral tracking.

4. Export, backup, and restore

You can export CSV data or create a JSON backup. A JSON backup may contain health records, profile information, and your avatar, so it should be stored securely. Restoring a backup replaces the current data in the app.

5. Data deletion

You can delete individual records from the record screen and remove the local avatar from the profile screen. Uninstalling the app removes its sandboxed data; whether a copy remains in device backups depends on your iOS settings.

6. System backups

LiteLog does not back up data to its own servers. Whether app data is included in device backups depends on your iOS backup settings and Apple services.

7. External links

The app may contain links to health reference or support websites. Those websites handle data according to their own privacy policies.
