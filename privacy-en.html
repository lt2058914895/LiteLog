Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

1. Local data processing

LiteLog is designed with a local-first approach. Weight, body fat, circumference, goals, nickname, and avatar data are stored by default in the app sandbox on your device. LiteLog does not operate a server that receives this health data or sell or share it.

2. iCloud backup

With LiteLog Pro, the app can automatically write a JSON backup to your own iCloud Documents container, and you can also start a manual backup or restore. Apple's iCloud service stores and syncs this data. The developer cannot access that container; processing is also governed by your Apple ID, iCloud settings, and Apple's privacy policy.

3. In-app purchases

LiteLog Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles payment, and LiteLog never receives or stores your card details. The app reads verified StoreKit transactions only to confirm Pro access and support purchase restoration.

4. Photos and avatar

Record photos are not supported. You can choose an avatar from the system photo library. The processed image is stored only in the app sandbox and protected by iOS file encryption.

5. Accounts and tracking

LiteLog does not create accounts, collect device identifiers, or include third-party advertising or behavioral tracking.

6. Export, backup, and restore

You can export CSV data or create a JSON backup. A JSON backup may contain health records, profile information, and your avatar, so it should be stored securely. Restoring a backup replaces the current data in the app.

7. Data deletion

You can delete individual records from the record screen and remove the local avatar from the profile screen. Uninstalling the app removes its sandboxed data. You can also manage iCloud backups in the Files app or iCloud settings; whether a copy remains in device backups depends on your iOS settings.

8. External links

The app may contain links to health reference or support websites. Those websites handle data according to their own privacy policies.
