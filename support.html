常见问题

数据保存在哪里？
当前版本的数据和头像仅保存在设备上的 App 沙盒中，不会上传到 LiteLog 服务器。

如何备份和恢复？
在设置页导出 JSON 备份，再通过“从备份恢复”导入。恢复会替换当前资料和记录。

如何删除数据？
可以在记录页删除单条记录，在个人资料页移除头像。卸载 App 会删除本地沙盒数据。

是否支持记录照片？
当前版本不支持记录照片，头像仍可在本地更换。

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Data and the avatar are stored only in the app sandbox. This version does not upload them to a LiteLog server.

How do I back up or restore?
Export a JSON backup from Settings and import it with Restore Backup. Restoring replaces the current profile and records.

How do I delete my data?
Delete records individually from the record screen and remove the avatar from the profile screen. Uninstalling the app removes its local sandbox data.

Does the app support record photos?
Record photos are not supported. A local avatar can still be changed from the profile screen.
