隐私政策

生效日期：2026年11月6日

1. 数据处理方式

LiteLog 采用本地优先设计。体重、体脂、围度、健康目标、昵称和头像等数据保存在用户设备的 App 沙盒中。当前版本不连接 LiteLog 后端，不上传、出售或共享这些数据。

2. 照片与头像

当前版本不支持为体重记录添加照片。用户可以从系统相册选择头像，处理后的头像文件仅保存在 App 沙盒中，并使用系统文件保护。

3. 账号与追踪

LiteLog 不创建账号，不采集设备标识，不包含第三方广告或行为追踪功能。

4. 导出、备份与恢复

用户可以在 App 内导出 CSV 数据或 JSON 备份。JSON 备份可能包含健康记录、个人资料和头像，请妥善保管。恢复备份会替换 App 中当前的资料和记录。

5. 数据删除

用户可以在记录页删除单条记录，并在个人资料页移除本地头像。卸载 App 会删除 App 沙盒中的数据；系统备份是否保留副本由用户的 iOS 设置决定。

6. 系统备份

LiteLog 本身不向服务器备份数据。用户设备上的系统备份是否包含 App 数据，由用户的 iOS 备份设置和 Apple 服务决定。

7. 外部链接

App 可能包含前往健康标准网站或支持页面的外部链接。打开链接后，相关网站的数据处理行为适用其自己的隐私政策。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

1. Local data processing

LiteLog is designed as a local-first app. Weight, body-fat, circumference, goals, nickname, and avatar data are stored in the app sandbox. This version does not connect to a LiteLog backend and does not upload, sell, or share this data.

2. Photos and avatar

Record photos are not supported. Users can choose an avatar from the system photo library. The processed image is stored only in the app sandbox and protected by iOS file encryption.

3. Accounts and tracking

LiteLog does not create accounts, collect device identifiers, or include third-party advertising or behavioral tracking.

4. Export, backup, and restore

Users can export CSV data or a JSON backup. A JSON backup may contain health records, profile information, and the avatar, so it should be stored securely. Restoring a backup replaces the current data in the app.

5. Data deletion

Users can delete individual records from the record screen and remove the local avatar from the profile screen. Uninstalling the app removes its sandboxed data; whether a copy remains in device backups depends on the user's iOS settings.

6. System backups

LiteLog does not back up data to its own servers. Whether app data is included in device backups depends on the user's iOS backup settings and Apple services.

7. External links

The app may contain links to health reference or support websites. Those websites handle data according to their own privacy policies.
