Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Your records and avatar are stored by default only in the app sandbox on your device. With Pro, you can also back up to your own iCloud container, which the developer cannot access.

How do I back up or restore my data?

Everyone can export or import a JSON backup from Settings. With Pro, you can also back up to iCloud immediately or restore the most recent iCloud backup. Restoring replaces the current profile and records.

What is LiteLog Pro?

LiteLog Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It includes automatic iCloud backup, Body Data Reports, and future Pro features. Core recording, statistics, and reminders remain free.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open LiteLog Pro in Settings and choose Restore Purchase. Make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles all in-app purchases. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com after signing in with your Apple ID. The developer cannot process refunds directly.

How do I create a Body Data Report?

With Pro enabled, open Body Data Report in Settings. After it is generated, use the system share sheet to save or send it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records from the record screen and remove your avatar from the profile screen. Uninstalling the app removes its local sandbox data. You can manage iCloud backups in the Files app or iCloud settings.

Does LiteLog support record photos?

Record photos are not supported in this version. You can still choose and change a local avatar from the profile screen.

Why do I need to back up before changing devices?

A JSON backup is the portable way to move records to another device. Pro users can also use the iCloud backup and restore flow when signed in to the same Apple ID.
