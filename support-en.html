Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Your records and avatar are stored only in the app sandbox on your device. This version does not upload them to a LiteLog server.

How do I back up or restore my data?

Open Settings, choose Export Data, and create a JSON backup. To restore, choose Restore Backup and select the JSON file. Restoring replaces the current profile and records.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records from the record screen and remove your avatar from the profile screen. Uninstalling the app removes its local sandbox data.

Does LiteLog support record photos?

Record photos are not supported in this version. You can still choose and change a local avatar from the profile screen.

Why do I need to back up before changing devices?

LiteLog does not use an account or cloud synchronization. A JSON backup is the supported way to move your records to another device.
